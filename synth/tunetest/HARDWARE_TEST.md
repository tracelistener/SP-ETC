What's new: the SY synth from test 1 now has 15 sounds. To pick one, push the VALUE knob in, keep it held, and tap a
pad. A popup shows the sound's number and name, and the pad's own sample doesn't play. Pad 16 switches the synth off,
and any other pad switches it back on.

| Pad | Sound | Pad | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Saw lead (test 1's) | 9 | Bell |
| 2 | Square | 10 | Brass |
| 3 | Pulse | 11 | Bass |
| 4 | Supersaw | 12 | Sweep |
| 5 | Pad | 13 | Wah |
| 6 | Strings | 14 | Seq: steps in time with the SP's tempo |
| 7 | Organ | 15 | Warm |
| 8 | Flute | 16 | Off |

As in test 1, it plays on every screen except the Sound Generator's, with EXT SOURCE on and ROUTING on ExtIn.

1. Volume low at first. Guitar in, EXT SOURCE on, input FX Bypass, ROUTING ExtIn, main screen.
2. Hold VALUE and tap pads 1 to 15, one at a time, playing a little on each.
3. Hold VALUE and tap pad 16: the synth stops. Hold VALUE and tap another pad: it comes back.
