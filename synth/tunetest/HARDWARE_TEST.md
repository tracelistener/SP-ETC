- **SG SYNTH** is SG PLUCK and SG FM BELL in one: CTRL 1 (WAVE) picks the Sound Generator wave, CTRL 2 (DUTY) its
  duty, the FILTER page its ENV. It starts as SG PLUCK (Pulse, DUTY 30, ENV Pluck).
- **Settings start fresh:** this set keeps its knobs in a new file, since the old one's settings belong to other
  sounds. The bus (OUT:) is back to DRY.

1. Play a few sounds from each bank: the lit pad follows.
2. With the guitar synth on (EXT SOURCE green), turn GAIN all the way up and strum hard: does EXT SOURCE blink red?
   Then set GAIN just below where it blinks.
